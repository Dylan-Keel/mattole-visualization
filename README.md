# Mattole Visualization

A registered aerial image time series for 440 Cooskie Ridge Road, Petrolia (APN 104-122-019, 163.6 acres) and the 494-acre Wild Turkey Creek Watershed it drains to, a tributary of the Mattole River.

**View it:** https://dylan-keel.github.io/mattole-visualization/

## What is in it

- Twenty-two dates from October 1941 to July 2025
- Three views: the watershed, the parcel, and a 3D view looking due south down the watershed
- Optional overlays: parcel boundary, watershed boundary, streams, and CAL FIRE historical fire perimeters
- Play, pause, zoom, and save any frame as a still

## Sources

- 1941 to 1981: scanned prints from the Humboldt County Aerial Photograph Collection, Cal Poly Humboldt
- 1993 and 1998: USGS National Aerial Photography Program (UCSB Library scans)
- 2005 to 2024: USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program
- 2025: Esri World Imagery
- Parcel boundary: Humboldt County GIS. Streams: National Hydrography Dataset. Elevation: USGS 3DEP 10 m. Fire perimeters: CAL FIRE

## Caveats

Historic frames were orthorectified for this project with fit residuals of about 7 to 12 m, so features shift slightly between years. The parcel boundary, watershed boundary and stream lines are not a survey. Wild Turkey Creek is the owner's name for an unnamed tributary.

## Files

`index.html` is the viewer. The `.jpg` files are the frames for each view and date.
